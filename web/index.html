How it works

The structure is downloaded from the RCSB PDB and parsed in the browser. Each chain's atoms become a union of spheres: a signed distance field sampled on a lattice in 32³ chunks. A pool of Web Workers triangulates the chunks in parallel with marching cubes.

A spatial hash over the atoms gives every vertex its nearest atom, which drives colouring and hover picking, and a smooth normal blended from nearby atom spheres. Screen-space ambient occlusion and per-chain outlines are added in post-processing.
